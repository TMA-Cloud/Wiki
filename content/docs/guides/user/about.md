**Settings** → **About** shows what you are running and where to go next. It is the same in the web app and the desktop app.

## What It Shows

- **The app**: its icon, name and version.
- **Platform**: the web app in your browser, or the desktop app with its system (for example _Desktop app on Windows (x86_64)_).
- **Server**: the address you are signed in to and the version it runs.

## Reporting a Problem

1. Open **Settings** → **About**.
2. Click **Copy details**. It copies your app version, platform and server version, but not the server's address.
3. Click **Report issue** and paste the details into a new issue, with what you did and what went wrong.
